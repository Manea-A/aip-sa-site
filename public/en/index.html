Questions

Before you ask.

Do you build private data centers for organizations?

Yes. We design, build and operate data centers to your needs, from edge sites inside your facility to AI-ready infrastructure, with optional ongoing operations and monitoring.

Do your solutions work with our current systems?

Yes. They work with the systems you already have, such as your website, WhatsApp, email, ERP, CRM and HR, with nothing to replace.

Where is our data hosted?

It depends on the solution and your needs: cloud, hosting inside the Kingdom, or your own servers, in line with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law.

How long does implementation take?

It depends on the scope. Ready platforms switch on faster; custom projects follow a clear plan: listen, design, build, measure.

Can we see a demo before deciding?

Of course. We arrange a demo and walk through a use case from your own operations.
